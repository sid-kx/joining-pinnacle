Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Introduction

This policy describes how Pinnacle Realty may handle information connected with this website, including education content, brokerage inquiries and scheduling. The information involved depends on how you use the site and which services you choose.

Information We Collect

Information you choose to provide may include your name, contact details and the contents of an inquiry. Website and service providers may also receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and device information. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial documents through general inquiries.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to requests, arrange conversations, operate and maintain the website, understand usage and help protect services against misuse. Other uses may require notice or consent, where applicable.

Cookies and Analytics

The website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for functionality, preferences, security or usage measurement. The technologies involved depend on the service used. Browser settings may let you limit or remove stored data, although some features may stop working.

Third-Party Services

Hosting, content delivery, fonts, embedded YouTube videos and scheduling tools may be supplied by third parties. Depending on how you interact with them, those providers may receive technical or submitted information and process it under their own privacy policies, including outside your location.

Contact Forms / Scheduling

If you submit an inquiry or book a conversation through a linked scheduling service, the information you provide may be used to manage that request and follow up with you. Review the service provider's privacy information before submitting details. Booking a call does not require you to provide sensitive transaction documents.

Data Security

Reasonable care should be taken when handling personal information. However, no website, network or storage system can guarantee complete security. Use appropriate care when sharing information online and notify Pinnacle Realty if you believe information related to this site has been misused.

Data Retention

Information may be retained as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, relevant administrative needs and applicable obligations. Retention depends on the information and service involved; third-party providers may apply their own practices.

Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to submit optional information, adjust browser settings or ask questions about information you have shared. Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent for certain uses. Some requests may be subject to identity verification, legal requirements or service limitations.

External Links

Links may take you to websites not operated by Pinnacle Realty. Their content, privacy practices and terms are separate from this policy. Review those policies when leaving this site.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general real estate and professional audience, not for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, contact Pinnacle Realty so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as the website or relevant practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision of this page. Additional notice may be provided where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Pinnacle Realty through the Schedule a Call option. Mention that your request concerns privacy so it can be directed appropriately.
